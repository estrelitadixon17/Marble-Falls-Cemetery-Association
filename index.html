The Sacred Duty of Remembrance

Marble Falls Cemetery Association isn't just a management group; we are the guardians of local history.

As a 501(c)(3) organization, our primary focus is the perpetual care of our cemetery grounds. This includes everything from meticulous lawn care and arboriculture to the stabilization of historic markers that have stood for over a century.

We believe every stone tells a story of the pioneers, veterans, and families who built the foundation of Marble Falls, Texas. Our nonprofit status ensures that every donation and plot sale is funneled directly back into the dignity and serenity of these grounds.

Preserving Our
Veterans' Graves

Special priority is given to the resting places of our service members. We host annual events to clean markers and place flags, ensuring their sacrifice is never forgotten under our watch.

  • Headstone Leveling & Restoration
  • Erosion Control & Landscaping
  • Historical Archiving of Grave Sites
