Our Commitment to Heritage

As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, our existence is fueled by community stewardship. We do not operate for profit; every dollar received via donations or plot sales is reinvested into the landscaping, security, and restoration of the Marble Falls Cemetery.

Community Legacy

We preserve the stories and memories of the families who built Marble Falls, ensuring their resting places remain dignified.

Perpetual Care

Our endowment fund ensures that maintenance continues long after current generations, protecting the landscape indefinitely.

Tax-Deductible Giving

As a 501(c)(3), your contributions are tax-deductible. We rely on your generosity to fund major restoration projects.

Support Our Mission

Your donations help us maintain irrigation, pave pathways, and restore weathered headstones. Together, we can keep the history of Marble Falls alive.
