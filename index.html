Who We Are

A Community of Care

The **Marble Falls Cemetery Association** is not just a management group; we are stewards of memory. As a registered **501(c)(3) nonprofit**, our work is fueled entirely by the devotion of community members and the generosity of families who wish to see their loved ones' resting places kept in pristine condition.

"Our goal is to provide a peaceful sanctuary for reflection, ensuring that every acre of these hallowed grounds reflects the respect our ancestors deserve."

Our Legacy

Preserving Marble Falls Since 1882

From the early Texas pioneers to the modern-day leaders of our city, the Marble Falls Cemetery holds the lineage of our region. Our association works tirelessly to:

  • ✓ Historic Documentation: Archiving burial records for future generations.
  • ✓ Land Restoration: Protecting the native Texas flora and ancient oaks.
  • ✓ Perpetual Care: Ensuring that no plot is ever forgotten or neglected.
Association Guidelines

Policies & Stewardship

1. Respectful Visitation

The grounds are open from sunrise to sunset. We ask all visitors to maintain a quiet atmosphere. Professional photography and commercial filming require written consent from the Association Board.

2. Monument & Marker Standards

To ensure the long-term stability of the grounds, all headstones and monuments must be approved before installation. This includes specifications on concrete footings to prevent shifting in the Texas soil.

3. Floral & Decoration Policy

We welcome fresh flowers and small seasonal tributes. However, to facilitate mowing and maintenance, any glass containers, lighting, or ground-stakes that pose a safety hazard will be removed by staff.

4. Privacy & Data Policy

Information shared with the Marble Falls Cemetery Association regarding plot ownership or genealogy is handled with strict confidentiality. We do not sell donor lists or personal family data.

5. 501(c)(3) Transparency

Financial reports and meeting minutes are available to members upon request. Your donations are fully tax-deductible as allowed by law under our nonprofit status.
