What is the Marble Falls Cemetery Association?

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation dedicated exclusively to the maintenance and preservation of the historic cemetery grounds in Marble Falls, Texas. Unlike for-profit memorial parks, our association is built upon a foundation of community stewardship.

Our primary purpose is the Perpetual Care of the final resting places of our citizens. This means that every dollar received through plot sales, donations, and community grants is reinvested directly into the land. From the meticulous grooming of the lawns and the health of our ancient oak trees to the restoration of century-old headstones, our association ensures that the dignity of these sacred grounds is never compromised.

Why Our Nonprofit Status Matters

Because we are a registered 501(c)(3) organization, your contributions are fully tax-deductible. This status holds us to the highest standard of financial transparency and accountability.

A Legacy Since 1882

The history of Marble Falls is literally written in the stones of our cemetery. From the pioneers who founded our city to the veterans who defended our nation, the Marble Falls Cemetery Association has stood as the guardian of these stories for over 140 years.

"To visit our cemetery is to walk through the timeline of the Texas Hill Country. Our association works tirelessly to ensure that as time marches on, the markers of our ancestors remain steadfast and honored."

Governance & Policies

Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. Any information submitted via our contact form or donation portal is used exclusively for association business. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third party. Our financial records regarding donations are kept secure and confidential according to IRS regulations.

Donation & Refund Policy

Donations made to the Marble Falls Cemetery Association are voluntary and considered charitable contributions. While we do not typically offer refunds for donations, if a technical error occurs during your transaction, please contact us within 30 days for assistance.

Public Accountability

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, our Form 990 is available for public inspection. We are governed by a Board of Directors who meet regularly to discuss maintenance schedules, budget allocations, and preservation projects.

Contact the Association

Whether you are inquiring about plot availability, looking for genealogical records, or wishing to make a tax-deductible donation, our board is here to help.
