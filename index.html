Who We Are

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to the perpetual care, maintenance, and historical preservation of the Marble Falls Cemetery. We serve as the stewards of local history, ensuring that the final resting places of our founders and loved ones remain beautiful and accessible for generations to come.


Our association is comprised of volunteers and community members who believe that a cemetery is more than just a plot of land; it is a living archive of the families that built Marble Falls.

Our Mission & Vision

Preservation

Restoring historical monuments and maintaining the natural landscape of our sacred grounds.

Community

Providing a peaceful space for reflection and a historical resource for family genealogists.

Education

Documenting the lives of those buried here to tell the story of the Texas Hill Country.

A Century of Remembrance

Established in the late 19th century, the Marble Falls Cemetery houses the pioneers of the granite industry, local veterans, and families that have called Texas home for over 150 years. As a nonprofit, every dollar donated goes directly toward the irrigation, security, and landscaping of these hallowed grounds.
