Our Mission

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to the perpetual care, maintenance, and beautification of our historic grounds. We believe that a cemetery is more than just a resting place; it is a living chronicle of our town’s history.

As a community-led association, we rely on the generosity of donors and volunteers to ensure that every grave is respected and every family finds peace when they visit.

A Legacy in Stone

Founded to serve the citizens of Marble Falls, TX, the association manages the historical documentation and physical upkeep of the cemetery plots. From the pioneers who built this city to the veterans who protected our freedom, our grounds hold the stories of those who came before us.

Our efforts include historical preservation of aging headstones, landscaping designed for Texas climates, and expansion planning for future generations.

Association Policies

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Any information collected during donations or inquiries is used solely for the administration of the Association and is never sold to third parties.

Rules of Conduct

Visitors are asked to remain on paths, keep pets leashed, and respect the solemnity of the grounds. No unauthorized landscaping or alterations to plots are permitted.

Flower Policy

Fresh flowers are welcome. Artificial arrangements will be removed seasonally or when they become weathered to maintain the natural beauty of the site.

Whether you are researching genealogy, inquiring about a plot, or looking to volunteer, we are here to help.
