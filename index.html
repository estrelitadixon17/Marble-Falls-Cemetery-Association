A Non-Profit Legacy

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is not a commercial venture. We are a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization serving as the perpetual stewards of our community's history. Our board consists of dedicated volunteers who receive no compensation—only the satisfaction of honoring our ancestors.


As a non-profit, we rely entirely on plot sales and tax-deductible donations to maintain the landscaping, infrastructure, and historical integrity of these grounds. Every dollar stays within the cemetery to ensure it remains a place of peace for the next century.

Our Non-Profit Pillars

Perpetual Care

Ensuring the grass, trees, and pathways are meticulously maintained through our specialized endowment fund.

Heritage Guard

Restoring damaged 19th-century headstones and monuments to protect the genealogical history of Marble Falls.

Open Records

Providing free access to burial records and maps for families and historical researchers across Texas.

Official Policies & Rules

501(c)(3) Disclosure & Governance

Donations to the Marble Falls Cemetery Association are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Our financial records are available for review by donors and members upon written request. We are governed by a volunteer Board of Trustees committed to transparency.

Grounds & Visitation Rules

  • Visitation: Strictly prohibited after sunset (Dawn to Dusk only).
  • Decorations: To facilitate maintenance, glass containers and permanent fences are not permitted. Artificial flowers must be removed when they become weathered.
  • Conduct: This is a sacred ground. Loud music, littering, and unauthorized commercial photography are strictly prohibited.

Privacy & Data Policy

We respect the privacy of our families and donors. We never sell, rent, or share personal contact information with third-party marketing firms. Information collected is used solely for plot records and nonprofit communication.
