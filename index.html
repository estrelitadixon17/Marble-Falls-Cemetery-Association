A Commitment to Endless Stewardship

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a volunteer-led effort. We understand that a cemetery is more than a piece of land; it is a park of memories, a place for genealogical research, and a sanctuary for the grieving.

Our focus includes the restoration of weathered headstones, the planting of native Texas flora to provide shade and beauty, and the digitalization of burial records to assist families in finding their loved ones.

We work closely with local veterans groups to ensure that every soldier's grave is recognized and honored with the respect they earned through service.

Legal Transparency

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Contributions are deductible to the extent permitted by law. We operate in accordance with the Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 712 regarding Perpetual Care Cemeteries.
