The Soul of Our Community

Located in the heart of Texas, the Marble Falls Cemetery Association serves as more than just a caretaker. We are the guardians of history. As a non-profit, our work is fueled by the love of the community and the respect we hold for those who came before us.


Every path we clear and every monument we restore ensures that the stories of our ancestors remain etched in the landscape of Marble Falls for generations to come.

Our Sacred Pillars

Vigilant Care

Year-round professional landscaping ensures a dignified resting place that reflects the beauty of the Texas Hill Country.

Historical Honor

Specialized restoration programs for century-old headstones and monuments to fight the wear of time and elements.

Ancestral Roots

Comprehensive digital and physical record-keeping to assist families in genealogy and finding their loved ones.

Visitor Guidelines

Hours

Dawn to Dusk. We maintain these hours to ensure the safety of our visitors and the integrity of the grounds.

Floral Tribute

Fresh flowers are always welcome. Artificial arrangements are moved seasonally to maintain the natural aesthetic of the park.

Preservation

To allow for professional mowing and care, we kindly ask that no glass containers or permanent fencing be installed at individual sites.

Tax Status

As a 501(c)(3), all donations are tax-deductible. Your support directly funds the beautification of these historic grounds.
