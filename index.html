Who We Are

Stewards of the Past, Guardians of the Future

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Unlike commercial cemeteries, we are driven entirely by a mission of respect rather than profit. Our association is governed by a dedicated volunteer board committed to ensuring that every burial site is maintained with the highest level of care.


We believe a community is defined by how it honors its ancestors. Through your plot purchases and tax-deductible donations, we fund the landscaping, road maintenance, and monumental restoration necessary to keep these grounds a sanctuary of peace.

Our Commitments

The Work of the Association

Perpetual Landscape Care

We manage year-round mowing, tree surgery, and pathway clearing to provide a safe and beautiful environment for visiting families.

Historical Restoration

Our "Heritage Fund" actively restores aging monuments and markers from the 19th century, preserving the genealogy of Marble Falls.

Public Records Access

We maintain an open-access digital and physical record of all interments, serving as a vital resource for historians and families.

Governance & Guidelines

501(c)(3) Nonprofit Disclosure

All contributions to the Marble Falls Cemetery Association are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Our annual financial filings and board meeting minutes are available for public inspection upon written request to the Board of Trustees. We are a non-stock, non-profit organization.

Cemetery Rules & Regulations

To maintain the solemnity of the grounds, the following rules are strictly enforced:

  • Hours: Dawn to Dusk. Trespassing after dark is strictly prohibited.
  • Decorations: Temporary floral arrangements only. Glass, permanent fencing, and solar lights are prohibited to allow for mechanical maintenance.
  • Conduct: Speed limits of 10mph are strictly enforced. Please respect the silence of visiting families.
Privacy Policy

We collect personal information (Name, Email, Phone) solely for the purpose of managing plot records and donation receipts. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third-party marketing firms. Our database is secured with industry-standard encryption.

Terms of Service

By using this website, you agree to respect the historical nature of the data provided. Unauthorized scraping of burial records for commercial use is strictly prohibited. All historical imagery is the property of the Association.
