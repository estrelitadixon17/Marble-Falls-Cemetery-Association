Our Identity

A 501(c)(3) Stewardship Since 1882

As a federally recognized nonprofit, our mission transcends business. We are the custodians of Marble Falls' heartbeat—preserving the final resting place of pioneers, veterans, and visionaries.

Perpetual Care & Dignity

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association ensures that every inch of our sacred ground is maintained with the highest standards of arboriculture and landscaping. Our EIN (81-5280617) stands as a badge of our commitment to public accountability and community service.

Our Community Initiatives

Veteran Honor

We coordinate with local VFW posts to ensure every veteran's grave is meticulously marked and honored during national holidays.

Green Preservation

Utilizing native Texas flora to create a self-sustaining ecosystem that provides a peaceful haven for families and wildlife.

Digital Archives

Free public access to digital burial records and historical documents to aid in genealogical research.

Financial Transparency

"As a 501(c)(3) entity, our books are as open as our gates."

Privacy & Data

We collect only essential data for donation receipts and plot management. We never share donor information with third-party solicitors. Your trust is our most valuable asset.

Donation Policy

All contributions to the Marble Falls Cemetery Association are tax-deductible. Funds are used exclusively for perpetual care, restoration, and historical archiving projects.
