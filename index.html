About the Association

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Unlike many modern memorial parks, we are not a commercial entity. We are a collection of community members and volunteers tasked with the sacred duty of managing and preserving the Marble Falls Cemetery.

Our status as a 501(c)(3) means that every single dollar we receive is directly reinvested back into the maintenance, security, and beautification of the cemetery grounds. We have no shareholders and no profit motives. Our only goal is to ensure that the individuals buried here are honored and that their resting places remain pristine for future generations to visit and pay their respects.

When you purchase a plot or make a donation, you are contributing to the Perpetual Care Fund. This fund is protected by Texas state law and association bylaws to ensure that even a hundred years from today, there will be resources available to mow the grass, trim the trees, and maintain the fences of this historic site.

A Foundation of
Community Heritage

"Established in 1882 to serve the pioneers of the Texas Hill Country."

The history of Marble Falls is deeply intertwined with the stones found within our gates. Since the late 19th century, this cemetery has served as the final sanctuary for the families who built this town. From the granite workers who helped construct the Texas State Capitol to the veterans of every American conflict, our grounds are a living archive.

The Association works closely with local historical societies to document and preserve grave markers that have suffered from the passage of time and the elements. We believe that by protecting these stones, we are protecting the very identity of Marble Falls itself.

Our Stewardship Duties

Transparency in Operation

Landscaping

Weekly lawn maintenance, seasonal planting of native Texas flora, and specialized tree care for our historic oaks.

Infrastructure

Maintenance of roadways, perimeter fencing, and drainage systems to prevent erosion of burial sites.

Record Keeping

Maintaining accurate plot maps and burial records to assist families and genealogists in their research.

The Marble Falls Cemetery Association is committed to protecting your privacy. Terms of Use & Visitation

By visiting the grounds, you agree to respect the peace and dignity of the cemetery. Visiting hours are from Sunrise to Sunset. No heavy machinery, unapproved markers, or unauthorized landscaping is permitted without prior written consent from the Association board.
